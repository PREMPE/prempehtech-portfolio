NETWORKING FUNDAMENTALS · PART 2

Networking Fundamentals for Beginners Part 2 | Local vs Remote, Routing & Client-Server

Part 2 explains how a host decides whether a destination is local or remote, why the IP prefix matters, and why routing is needed between separate networks. It then introduces internetworking, clients, servers, network services and resources, request-and-response communication, client-server architecture, peer-to-peer networking, and a practical troubleshooting path.

NETWORKING FUNDAMENTALS · PART 3

Networking Fundamentals Part 3 | Packets, Frames, Encapsulation & Network Performance

Part 3 follows data as it moves across a network. It covers bits and bytes, protocol data units, IP packets, headers and payloads, Ethernet frames, encapsulation and decapsulation, physical transmission over copper, fiber, and wireless, and the key performance concepts of bandwidth, latency, throughput, packet loss, and jitter.
